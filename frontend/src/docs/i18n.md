# Internationalization

`i18n::I18n` holds an app's messages in catalogs, one per locale, and the locale on show. The app sets it as a GPUI global; views read their words from it.

## Catalogs and messages

| Call | Does |
| --- | --- |
| `I18n::new("en-US")` | Starts with the locale on show. |
| `.catalog(tag, &[(key, message)])` | Adds a locale's messages. Every catalog holds the first one's keys, no more and no fewer, or it panics naming them. |
| `cx.set_global(i18n)` | Makes it the app's. |
| `cx.global::<I18n>().text(key, &[(name, value)])` | The message for `key`, each `{name}` filled. A missing key, argument or catalog panics. |
| `I18n::set_locale(tag, cx)` | Shows another locale in every window, logs the change, and panics on a locale without a catalog. |

Ely's components take their words from the app: a `Button`'s label, a `Dialog`'s title. Some carry English words of their own, such as a toast's Undo, the Cancel of `ConfirmDialog` and `PromptDialog`, and the Close tip on a fullscreen dialog.

## Locales

`LOCALES` lists the eleven locales Ely formats for: en-US, en-GB, es-ES, de-DE, fr-FR, ja-JP, ko-KR, vi-VN, zh-CN, zh-TW and he-IL. `Locale::of(tag)` returns one and panics on a tag outside the list.

| Item | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| `tag`, `name` | `"de-DE"`, `"Deutsch"`. |
| `number(value, decimals)` | The value with the locale's separators: `number(1234.5, 1)` is `1.234,5` in de-DE. |
| `date(at, zone)` | A numeric date in CLDR's short form. |
| `direction` | `Direction::Ltr` or `Direction::Rtl`. |

## Right to left

The he-IL locale reads right to left. `direction.row(element)` lays a flex row from the reading side, and `direction.align(element)` aligns text to that side.

GPUI sets no paragraph direction, so a line runs the way its first strong letter does. `text` leads a right-to-left message with a right-to-left mark, so its words suit labels, not a field's value. `number` leads a right-to-left minus with a left-to-right mark, as CLDR does. GPUI wraps right-to-left lines in the wrong order, so keep such text on one line, an `Ellipsis`.
